Isabella Bock
Yale University Computer Science

I am an PhD researcher at Yale University working with Brian Scassellati in the Social Robotics Lab. Previously I worked with Elaine Short in The Agent Assistive Behavior and Learning Lab (AABL) as an undergraduate researcher. My research interest is in Human-Robot Interaction (HRI), specifically in improving accessibility by developing robotic algorithms that encourage trust and communication with users.
